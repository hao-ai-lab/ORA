# Synthetic Qualification Boundary v1

The exact episode task is the objective. Current policy-visible observations
are authoritative for the qualification scene.

The public boundary exposes selected rendered sensors, calibration and robot
proprioception. Hidden simulator state, contacts, object ground-truth poses,
success predicates and implementation details are private and unavailable.

Synthetic qualification does not provide an official Boolean success verdict.
Tool completion and policy lifecycle reports establish only their documented
local result.
