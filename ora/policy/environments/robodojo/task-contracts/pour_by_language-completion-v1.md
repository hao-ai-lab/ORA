Public completion contract for this task:

- Each bottle's liquid must go into the bowl the instruction pairs it with, the
  bottles and bowls being told apart by the colours the instruction names: the
  first bottle named into the first bowl named, the second into the second and
  the third into the third. Liquid poured into any other bowl does not count for
  that bottle.
- A bottle's pour counts only when just a little of its liquid is left in it and
  almost none of the liquid that has left it lies anywhere but inside its paired
  bowl. Small droplets lying scattered apart from the rest of that liquid are
  disregarded, but if such scattered droplets add up to a sizeable share of it,
  that pour does not count.
- Each bottle's pour is judged at the moment that bottle comes back close to
  upright, neck up, after having been tipped away from upright, whether it is
  still held or already set down. Its pour must already count at that moment; if
  it does not, only a later fresh tip and return to upright of that bottle is
  judged. A bottle left tipped, lying on its side or upside down is never
  judged. A pour once judged to count stays counted, and the task is complete,
  and the episode ends, as soon as all three pours have been judged to count;
  the arm reset named at the end of the instruction is therefore never judged.
- The instruction lists the pours in order, first to third, and that order is
  checked whenever an arm returns to its reset pose. If at that moment a bowl
  named later in the instruction holds enough of its bottle's liquid for that
  pour to count while a bowl named earlier does not, the episode ends at once as
  a permanent failure that nothing done afterwards can undo. An earlier bowl
  whose liquid has since been spilled or knocked out counts as not holding it.
- This task does not require the arms to end at their reset poses.
