Public completion contract for this task:

- All three blocks must end up in one straight row, with their centres lying on
  a single straight line.
- Each block must also be squared to that row, its sides running along and
  straight across the line of the row; a block left twisted at an angle to the
  row does not count, even if its centre is on the line.
- Every block must stay down on the table the whole time: if any block is ever
  lifted off the table or raised up onto anything, even briefly, the episode
  ends at once as a permanent failure, and setting it back down does not undo
  it.
- Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the
  same instant that everything else in this contract is true.
