Public completion contract for this task:

- Swapping means that each block ends up on the mat where the other block stood
  when the episode reset. The two blocks look alike, so the finished arrangement
  looks the same as the start; it counts only if each block has actually been
  moved to the other's mat.
- The official check looks for exactly three moves, one after another: first one
  block is moved from its own mat onto the mat that was empty at the reset; then
  the other block is moved onto the mat the first block came from; and last the
  first block is moved from the formerly empty mat onto the mat the second block
  came from. Either block may go first. A move is finished only when its block
  has been set down on the middle of its new mat while the other block rests on
  its mat.
- A move counts only if its block is lifted clearly up off the mats on the way;
  a block slid, pushed or carried only just above the mats does not count as
  moved.
- After each move is finished, the button must be pressed once: pushed firmly
  down, then let go so that it springs back up, before the next move begins. The
  task is complete once the button has sprung back up after the press that
  follows the third move.
- The button may be pressed only three times in the whole episode, once after
  each move. Every press counts toward that limit whenever it is made, including
  one made before a move is finished or an extra press to be sure, and a fourth
  press ends the episode at once as a permanent failure; letting go of the
  button afterwards does not undo it.
- Both arms must be back at the poses they held when the episode reset, at the
  same instant that everything else in this contract is true.
