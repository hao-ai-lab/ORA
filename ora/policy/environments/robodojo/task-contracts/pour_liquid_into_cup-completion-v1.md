Public completion contract for this task:

- Pour the bottle's liquid into the cup: only a little of it may be left in the
  bottle, and almost none of the liquid that has left the bottle may lie
  anywhere but inside the cup.
- Small droplets lying scattered apart from the rest of the liquid are
  disregarded, but if such scattered droplets add up to a sizeable share of the
  liquid, the pour does not count.
- The pour is judged at the moment the bottle comes back close to upright, neck
  up, after having been tipped away from upright, whether it is still held or
  already set down. Everything above must already be true at that moment; if it
  is not, only a later fresh tip and return to upright is judged. A bottle left
  tipped, lying on its side or upside down is never judged, however much liquid
  is in the cup. The task is complete, and the episode ends, at the first such
  moment at which everything above is true.
- This task does not require the arms to end at their reset poses.
