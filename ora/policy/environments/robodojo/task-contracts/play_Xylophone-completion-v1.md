Public completion contract for this task:

- Left to right runs from world -X, the side of the table where the left arm is
  mounted, toward world +X, the right-arm side. Every key must be struck, one
  after another in the order the keys lie along the xylophone, beginning with
  the key at its left end and ending with the key at its right end.
- A key counts as struck when the head of the mallet comes down to that key,
  touching it or just above it, over the key itself rather than between keys or
  beside the xylophone. Contact by any other part of the mallet, or by a
  gripper, does not count.
- After each strike the mallet must be lifted clearly back up from that key
  before a strike on the next key counts; sliding the mallet along the keys
  without lifting it counts as a single strike at most.
- A key counts only once every key to its left has been struck in turn; a key
  struck out of turn must be struck again later. The task is complete at the
  moment the key at the right end is struck.
- This task does not require the arms to end at their reset poses.
